Record meetings in bb and keep an editable notepad beside your work. Margins saves recordings and transcripts in your selected Margins Workspace and can open a thread to make a connected note from a saved meeting.

## What you get

- Microphone recording from the bb browser on desktop or mobile.
- A shared meeting notepad that stays with the recording.
- A connected-note action for saved meetings.
- Pairing with Margins.app on a Mac for microphone and computer audio.

## Requirements

The project host must be an Apple Silicon Mac or x86-64 Linux machine. On first use, the plugin installs the matching, verified `margins` and `margins-server` release binaries on that host. For computer audio, install Margins.app separately on the recording Mac and connect it from **Meetings** in bb. Browser recording uses the microphone only.
