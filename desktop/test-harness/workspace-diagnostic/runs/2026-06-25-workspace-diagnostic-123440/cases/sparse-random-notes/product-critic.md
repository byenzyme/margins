# Product Critic: sparse-random-notes

## Strongest thing the diagnostic did
Picked the conservative `notes/meetings/` destination over the tempting
`clients/Acme/` and `projects/Orbit/` folders, and explicitly listed "create
people folders without evidence" and "move notes into client folders" as
do-not-do. That is exactly the right restraint for a personal, mixed-by-habit
vault.

## Most serious trust risk
The "What Is Uncertain" section leaks convention-tag jargon ("metadata signals
are inline-tags, body-labels") instead of translating to receptive language. A
first-timer reads tag soup, not reassurance. Low severity, but recurring.

## Would the user feel ready to start capture?
Yes. Capture-now and non-mutation are explicit.

## Is import/history understood as optional?
Yes, but via the generic boilerplate line. It names transcripts but does not
specifically reassure that the plain-text Zoom transcript stays untouched.

## Surface / patch implicated
skill: translate convention tags into plain-language uncertainty. Indexability
lens is underused — uncertainty echoes the manifest rather than reasoning about
which existing notes are usable retrieval signal.
